# NBA transition — discovery and paper-only research brief

Initial inventory observed 2026-10-05 UTC; score-history snapshot observed 2026-10-06 UTC. This documents an **experimental scores-only NBA baseline**, not a validated edge or pick list. Separate uncommitted NFL Risk Allowance edits are excluded from this NBA-only release; do not generalize their thresholds to NBA.

## 2026-10-21 update: score-only v2 and pregame diagnostics

### Parallel higher-hit-rate paper shadow (forward-only)

The existing top-study ranking **still includes totals and is unchanged**. For newly scanned games only, a separate ML/spread shadow may be recorded alongside a deliberately tracked top study; it is not another real or paper-ledger stake and never replaces a frozen total or other primary contract. The challenger must have a strictly higher experimental model hit estimate than the top study, at least 55% model probability, at least 3 percentage points of estimated probability above all-in cost, and a quoted all-in cost no more than 1/1.7 per contract (at least 1.7× gross *estimated* payout). These are provisional research gates, **not calibrated confidence or a profitable-bet guarantee**. Spread shadows require a sourced pre-tip sportsbook spread and a Kalshi line within three points. Beyond the original four model-nearest spreads, at most four additional sportsbook-near spread books are inspected **only for the shadow**; no claim that every listed line was priced. Missing reference, sized depth, fair pricing, or a qualifying higher-probability alternative means no shadow.

When the primary is explicitly tracked before tip, the frozen record preserves both exact tickers/sides, rules, quotes, books, model probabilities and observation times. Final shadow resolution is retrieved independently from the exact Kalshi contract and official score and appended as a separate immutable audit; an unsettled or mismatched contract remains pending/needs review, not an inferred win. History displays shadow W/L and hypothetical net units separately from the baseline 1u ledger, so same-game alternatives never inflate the actual paper-pick record. Existing freezes are not backfilled or reinterpreted. ESPN signed favorite spreads are now accepted when the absolute value reconciles to the named favorite; this fixes a missing independent-reference case and can alter *new* scan inputs even though the primary ranking rule is unchanged.

The dated v1 study below describes the original opening-slate implementation and is preserved as historical context. The current selected-date scan and matchup page now refresh **final 2026–27 regular-season scores** from [ESPN monthly scoreboards](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/basketball/nba/scoreboard?dates=202610&limit=500) at request time, requiring a final state, exact NBA team IDs and scores and a six-hour lag. The earlier 2024–26 snapshot and 2024 residual calibration remain unchanged. `nba-scores-v2` identifies the refreshed inputs, **not a validated new coefficient or demonstrated edge**. Unavailable/malformed monthly scores abort the scan rather than falling back to stale inputs. The frozen decision retains the version, forecast, observed history time, number of current-season finals and source URLs; previous freezes are not recalculated.

The [ESPN game summary](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/basketball/nba/summary?event=401909088) contains shot-attempt locations. The new research-only shot profile uses each team's last 2–3 finalized current-season games, reconciles FGA and 3PA to official box scores, and contrasts 3PA/paint-proxy/midrange-proxy *attempt shares* against opponent defensive allowed shares. ESPN coordinate-based paint and midrange **do not reconcile to official points in the paint**, so these are approximate descriptive zones with **zero numerical forecast weight**. Missing/invalid or too-small samples remain unknown. No player-return effect is quantified; dated injury listings and model–sportsbook spread disagreement remain human-review evidence, not fitted adjustments.

When ESPN exposes a pre-tip provider's `pickcenter.overUnder`, the scanner freezes its spread, total, provider, URL and read time separately from the selected Kalshi contract. It also inspects up to four listed total lines nearest that external reference (in addition to four nearest the score model). If the highest estimated model–cost gap is a total over six points from the sportsbook total, a nearer (within three points) *priced* total may replace it **only if** its estimated net gap remains positive and within five probability points of the highest candidate. These thresholds are unvalidated safeguards, not optimization to the first ten results. No reference, no near positive-value quote, or materially worse quote means no automatic switch. Prices, fee estimate, actual contract, source timing and uncertainty remain visible; no actual order path was added. Judge this policy on later paper slates and benchmark against the selected quote/market, not just observed wins.

## Implementation status

The NBA tab and authenticated `/api/nba-research` route fetch ESPN's [2025–26 regular-season standings](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/v2/sports/basketball/nba/standings?season=2026&seasontype=2) and [2026–27 schedule](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/basketball/nba/scoreboard?dates=20261020), enforce a 30-team complete baseline, and display dated side-by-side prior-season W–L/points-for/points-against for a selected game plus a filterable league table. These **are not possession-adjusted ratings** and account for neither roster changes nor lineup matchup effects. Six selected 2026 trades/signings checked against individual NBA.com reporting are displayed for relevant matchups (`src/nba-offseason.ts`); no complete transaction or lineup archive is yet present. The experimental score-only model supplies margin/total distributions and moneyline probabilities, with half-point spread/total calculations on manually entered lines. Dated prior head-to-head games are visible as context. The opening-slate Kalshi study scan and NBA browser-local freeze/review are separate from NFL. No NBA qualified paper pick is claimed. The NFL tab keeps its historical ledger and rules.

## October 20 Kalshi study checklist (local, 2026-10-08)

- The authenticated, GET-only `/api/nba-scan?date=2026-10-20&quantity=10` verifies all three ESPN game IDs, home/away team IDs, regular-season status and scheduled tip against the opening slate before mapping exact `KXNBAGAME`, `KXNBASPREAD`, `KXNBATOTAL` event identities. It reads all open pages of these three **public, unauthenticated** Kalshi series; no API key, account access or trading endpoint is used.[2][11]
- Winner contracts are identified by exact team ticker and rule text. Half-point full-game spread and total market rules are parsed strictly if listed; unsupported/unmatched rules are ignored, not guessed. For each game, the scanner prices YES and NO at **ten whole contracts per side** from opposite bid depth on two winner contracts and up to four nearest spread and four nearest total lines, rejecting insufficient book depth. It assumes the series's observed taker multiplier of 1 and the July 2026 general fee formula only as a **fee estimate**; the exact transaction fee, fill, and fair-price settlement are not verified.[2][7][12]
- The scanner ranks an **unqualified study side** by experimental score-model probability minus estimated all-in cost, including a negative best score if all sides look bad. If no independent model or priced book exists, it produces no study side. It never emits a qualified paper pick. The panel lists game-by-game coverage, ticker/side/rule text, observation time and open evidence gates: injuries, starters, opponent-adjusted offense/defense, pace, three-point profile, assists/chemistry, roster/rest context, final fees and contract resolution. The separate browser-local freeze preserves each of the three scan decisions plus raw mapped contract metadata and inspected books; a scan itself remains ephemeral until frozen before tip.
- The freeze requires a fresh (at most 30 minutes old) three-game scan, pre-tip schedule, ten-contract book, independent model, positive *estimated* net cost gap, operator-supplied dated (<24h) availability/lineup source and notes, rules/fee source URLs, and explicit rationale/uncertainty acknowledgment to label a requested side `PAPER_EXPERIMENT`. Otherwise it saves `STUDY` with reasons. URLs and notes are operator attestations **not automatically verified evidence**. Raw score model lacks actual lineup adjustment and the fee/fill estimate can be wrong, so `PAPER_EXPERIMENT` is not a qualified edge. The app does not force one per game or silently upgrade a study lead.
- Pre-tip freezes are appended to `edgeboard-nba-freezes-v1` in browser localStorage and can be exported with their separate `edgeboard-nba-reviews-v1` records as JSON. Users add the top study side from each game to a draft paper slip; only explicitly selected, pre-tip frozen sides enter the W/L tracker. Each is a 1-unit stake equal to its own ten-contract *estimated* all-in cost; net units after sourced YES/NO/fair-price settlement are `(selected-side payout / frozen all-in cost) - 1`. A loss is −1u; a win depends on entry price. Older $25/unit audit records remain immutable and are normalized only for tracker display. No actual fill is claimed. The append-only behavior is at the application layer, not tamper-proof or server-backed; browser deletion loses the record. See README's October 19 runbook.
- On the live read-only October 8 smoke test, each game had two winner contracts, no October 20 spread/total contracts, and a sized study comparison. A live scan also successfully assembled a three-game `STUDY`-only freeze snapshot in memory; no operator evidence or local browser decision was manufactured. All three qualified-paper flags were false. Live coverage can change and must be rescanned closer to tip. No deployment or real trade resulted.

## Score-only NBA baseline v1 (local experiment)

- `scripts/refresh_nba_games.py` downloads the 2024, 2025 and 2026 [SportsDataverse ESPN-derived NBA schedules](https://github.com/sportsdataverse/sportsdataverse-data/releases/tag/espn_nba_schedules), verifies 30 real NBA team identifiers against the ESPN standings feed, excludes mislabeled All-Star and Cup final exhibitions, and **requires exactly 1,230 completed regular-season games each year**. `server/data/nba-games.json` contains 3,690 records with UTC tip, home/away IDs, final scores, neutral status, source URLs, source SHA-256 hashes and observation time. ESPN/SportsDataverse result data is a historical archive, not a pregame injury/odds archive. Rebuild only with a fresh source-integrity review; do not overwrite a frozen prior forecast or pretend a later refresh existed at an earlier decision time.
- `server/nba-model.ts` uses only games more than six hours before the target tip, a contemporaneous league points baseline from at most 800 recent games, each team's last 30 games weighted by season and shrunk toward league mean, and a bounded league-wide home margin fit to 2024. It fits margin/total residual standard deviations to chronologically predicted 2024 games and applies a normal approximation. Teams with sparse history abstain. ML uses a half-point win threshold; home handicap is **home score + entered spread > away score**. Half-point spread and total lines only; integer lines can push and are not modeled. This is not a possession-based, opponent-adjusted, roster-aware or injury-aware NBA model.
- `tests/api/nba-model.test.ts` evaluates 2025–26 chronologically against frozen 2024 calibration: **2,460 score forecasts**, Brier **0.2111** (versus naïve 50/50 **0.25**), absolute margin error **11.33 points**, absolute total error **15.00 points**. These are score/probability diagnostics, **not net units, ROI, or proof of betting edge**. No decision-time sportsbook prices, Kalshi fees/depth, injury snapshots or qualified picks were in that test. Do not optimize thresholds on these outcomes and call them forward picks.
- `recentNbaMeetings` reports at most eight dated meetings from the preceding two seasons and shows which team was home, scores and neutral-site status. The selected matchup also shows each team's 2025–26 home or road W–L and average score margin, excluding neutral games. These contextual views have **zero extra numerical effect**: raw H2H samples are low, raw venue splits conflate team strength and opponent schedule, and rosters change. A future recency/roster-shrunk candidate must improve a separate subsequent holdout before any small, capped adjustment is allowed. Home court enters as a **league average only**, with no raw team home-record multiplier; neutral-site games get zero home adjustment.
- The first 2–3 weeks of 2026–27 are a high-uncertainty **paper-learning block**, not a commitment to place or simulate a pick each day. The frozen preseason snapshot abstains for game dates more than 21 days after its observed time, rather than claiming in-season freshness. Capturing prospective availability, quotes and outcomes is still required before any selection policy or net-return analysis.

## Grounded inventory

- The NBA lists October 20 as the first day of the 2026–27 regular season; preseason began October 3. Its opening slate is BOS–DET, PHI–NYK, and OKC–SAS.[1]
- Unauthenticated Kalshi public REST documents series, markets, events, orderbooks, and cursor pagination.[2] A read-only API scan found `KXNBAGAME` (72 open contracts, including six Oct 20 winner outcomes across those three games), `KXNBASPREAD` (200 open contracts), and `KXNBATOTAL` (168 open contracts). The spreads and totals in those complete open-series responses were dated Oct 5/6 (preseason), **none Oct 20** at observation time. The counts are time-specific, not a claim about future availability.[3][4][5]
- An actual Oct 20 `KXNBAGAME-26OCT20OKCSAS-SAS` contract resolves Yes on a San Antonio win in the *originally scheduled* game; a postponement begun within 48 hours uses the official result, otherwise the market can resolve to a **fair price**, not a simple loss/refund. Do not infer the true event cutoff from `close_time`; verify schedule, actual start, and the exact resolution rules. Its observed volume was nonzero, but neither volume nor displayed bids guarantees a fill.[6]
- The game series lists the Governing League and ESPN as settlement sources, and `quadratic_with_maker_fees` as its fee type. Do not transplant NFL fee assumptions or treat a bid as a purchase ask; compute size-specific executable YES/NO costs and applicable fees from the actual series and book.[7][2]
- ESPN's October 20 scoreboard response currently lists three opening games with UTC starts; compare against league schedule and original event identity, never match a contract only by a fuzzy team abbreviation.[11][1]

## Data availability and limitations

- `hoopR` documents bulk NBA play-by-play and ESPN live box/score access. Candidate source for prior-game scores, pace, possessions, and results; first check accessible dataset versions, identifiers, completeness, timestamps, reuse terms, and update reliability before using it in a model.[9]
- The official NBA injury-report process requires pregame and game-day updates and continuously changes. The located official page documents the **2025–26** season; a 2026–27 machine-readable, historically timestamped feed has **not** been confirmed. Injury/availability notes are contextual until a frozen, historically reproducible adjustment is prospectively tested.[8]
- The Odds API advertises timestamped historical NBA bookmaker snapshots but says historical access requires a paid subscription. That is a potential independent benchmark, **not an available integration** here. A free ESPN odds endpoint may expose pregame quotes, but we have not established bookmaker update timestamps or reliable historical time-of-decision snapshots; a closing line must not masquerade as a pregame quote.[10]
- No Kalshi historical depth at past decision times or NBA injury/lineup timestamp archive was verified. A backtest from scores and closing odds alone cannot prove an executable Kalshi edge. Start collecting forward snapshots, including rejected candidates and no-pick days.

## Proposed market qualification and prospective evaluation (not yet implemented)

1. **Scope:** October 20 onward, regular-season *full-game winner only*, both sides of each exact game contract. Quarantine preseason, Cup-special rules, spreads/totals, half/quarter, player props, future/champion markets, and unmatched events until separately mapped and validated. The October 5/6 preseason markets are useful for parser/rule diagnostics, not evidence of a regular-season edge.
2. **Collection:** read-only series-scoped pagination; preserve raw contract metadata, rule text, UTC retrieval time, endpoint/cursor, original event schedule and separately sourced tip time. At decision time fetch full bid books, calculate executable price for the frozen whole-contract size on the chosen side, and record fee schedule/version, fee estimate, depth, and stale/partial-fill rejection. Never add order submission, cancel, or amend capability.
3. **Independent market reference:** no Kalshi price as the independent forecast. Compare the experimental score-only baseline with a dated, independently sourced sportsbook price converted to no-vig probability **only if quote timestamp and event match are trustworthy**. Injuries remain contextual until quantified and tested. Freeze model version, training cutoff, available roster context, reference price and forecast before tip. Do not assume the NFL score residuals, A/B/C/D gates, or 52% modeled-chance threshold transfer to NBA.
4. **Decision:** separately label sourced, fully cost/depth/rule-qualified `PAPER` decisions versus unverified `Worth checking` leads; no fabricated picks. Sizing starts with the existing $25-per-unit accounting vocabulary, but define NBA-specific bankroll, per-game and portfolio exposure caps, correlation handling, and eligibility before the first prospective play. Abstain if the independent quote, rules, fee, executable book, or timing is unavailable.
5. **Settlement/evaluation:** preserve initial raw observation and pregame decision; source official result and handle fair-price/cancel/postponement and corrections. Report **net units after modeled fees/costs** (`net dollars / $25`), realized ROI, max drawdown, displayed-depth/fillability, sample size, probability calibration and Brier against executable Kalshi/independent sportsbook benchmarks. Win rate is secondary; a 52% target is not sufficient for positive units. Compare chronologically by game/date; no random split, same-game alternate-line leakage, or gate tuning on already observed outcomes. No claim of edge until a prospective multi-slate block is settled and uncertainty is reported.

## Release blockers

- Confirm regular-season contract coverage and exact rule variants closer to October 20; today only full-game winners were present for the opening regular-season slate.[3][4][5]
- Confirm legally usable historical result/odds/availability feeds, data quality, and a truly decision-time independent quote archive; resolve whether paid historical odds are needed.[8][9][10]
- Verify series-specific fee and book normalization for NBA with fixture tests and a fresh read-only smoke check; reject unfillable size and partial liquidity.[2][7]
- Keep NBA browser-local frozen scans/reviews isolated from the NFL ledger; a tamper-evident, server-backed or shared-device ledger and a versioned lineup-aware forecast are **not** implemented. Keep unfinished NFL risk work unshipped.
